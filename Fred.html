Fred is an avarage frog from Cathlamet, WA. He spends most of his days swimming in the river and eating flies. But at night, he puts on his cowboy costume and picks up a banjo and plays some good old fashioned bluegrass. He is a very talented frog, and has a great sense of humor. Give him some pets! Suck on his belly! He loves it when you rub his webbed feet and tell him how handsome he is. I think fred is a little like all of us.
