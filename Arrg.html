Free Stuff is like super cool, and I want you to get fre stuff too, here's some resources I use to get free stuff TOTALLY LICALLY.

  • Free Media Heck Yeah(FMHY)

    FMHY is a great website for finding online free streaming for movies and TV shows. Aswell as torrenting and downloading stuff you totally own. I use it for finding websites that are hosting new movies that just came out on streaming sites.

  • Libgen

    Libgen is a database of free scientific and literary publications. I've used it for finding textbooks and digital copies of books I totally already own physically.

  • Open Library

    The Open Library is a website that has over 3,000,000 free ebooks. They are all public domain, so they are all totally legal to download and read. I use it for finding classic literature and other books.

  • Internet Archive

    The Internet Archive is a website that archives web pages and other digital content. I've gotten leaked music releases from there. Aswell as books and TV shows!

  • The Servarr Apps

    • The Servarr Apps are a collection of apps that help you manage your media library. They include Sonarr for TV shows, Radarr for movies, Lidarr for music, and Readarr for books. They are all free and open source, and they are all great for finding and downloading media that you totally already own. Make sure you have a VPN!
