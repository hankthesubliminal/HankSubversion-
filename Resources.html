  • Streaming Services w/ no Ads?!?

    If you use firefox with the ublock origin extension, you can enjoy ad-free streaming on many platforms such as youtube, spotify and soundcloud. This is a great way to support artists you like without having to pay for a subscription.

  • Soulseek:

    I use the p2p(peer to peer) music sharing app called Soulseek(I highly reccomend the use of a VPN when using). It's good at sharing music i "own" to people who want it and vice versa. If you decide to get it, check out my profile @bigmanhenderson and check out the website aswell as the reddit page and forum.

  • Aimp:

    Aimp is a music player for windows. I use it because it's easy to use, customisable, has a build in tag editor and cd ripping software. It also has support for flac and other lossless formats. I've had it for years and its been great. I highly reccomend it to anyone who listens to music on their computer. I'll put the download link here.

  • Bandcamp:

    Bandcamp is a music streaming and purchasing platform that allows artists to sell their music directly to fans. I use it to discover new music and support artists I like. It's a great alternative to streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music, as it allows artists to earn more money from their music. I highly reccomend it to anyone who wants to support independent artists and discover new music. I'll put the link here.

  • Jellyfin:

    Jellyfin is a free and open-source media server software that allows you to organize, manage, and stream your media collection. I use it to host my music collection and stream it to my phone. You can check it out here.

  • Cover Search Engine(COV):

    COV is a search engine for album covers. I use it to find high quality album covers for my music collection. It's a great resource for finding album art that is not available on streaming services or other music platforms. You can check it out here.

  • Cosmos:

    Cosmos is a alternative to pintrest. It;s an image sharing platform that allows you to discover and share images. I use it to find insparation for art. It's a great resource for finding visual content related to anything you might want. You can check it out here.
